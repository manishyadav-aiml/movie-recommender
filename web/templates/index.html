        Frequently asked questions

        How does it work?

        It combines item-based collaborative filtering (people who liked this also liked that), genre similarity and a small popularity boost.

        What does the similarity score mean?

        It is a 0–100% match between the movie you chose and each suggestion, blended from the three signals above. Higher is closer.

        Is my data stored?

        Your taste profile and theme choice live in your browser only. We store a message only if you send us feedback.

        Where does the data come from?

        MovieLens, published by GroupLens Research at the University of Minnesota.
